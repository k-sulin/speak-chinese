How it works: the character speaks the questions, as in the exam, and you answer by speaking (or typing). The question text appears after you answer. No feedback is given during the conversation, as in the exam. Your answers are sent to an AI to be marked, and nothing is saved on a server; audio is not recorded.
你可以说话或打字回答。聊天过程中不给反馈（和考试一样）。你的回答会发送给AI批改；服务器不保存任何内容，也不录音。

The mark is an estimate made from a text transcript. It cannot judge pronunciation or tones, and the real exam mark may differ.
